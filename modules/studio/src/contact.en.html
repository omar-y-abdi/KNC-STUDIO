Contact Blade & Blend Studio

Book or manage an appointment

To make a new booking, use our official booking page. There you can choose an available barber, treatment and time. Prices and availability are shown in the booking flow and may change. If you already have a booking, you can open My bookings to manage it with your own access.

Information before your visit

Read the booking terms before you book, especially the information about cancellation and contact. The privacy policy describes how we handle name, phone number, email and other information used when you book. Contact the salon if you need help finding the right information.
