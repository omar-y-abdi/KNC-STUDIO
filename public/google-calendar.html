Blade & Blend Studio

Staff booking calendar integration

Blade & Blend Studio is a public barbershop and online appointment-booking service in Gothenburg, Sweden. Customers can browse services, choose a barber, and book or manage an appointment without a Google account or staff login.

Purpose of Google Calendar access

Authenticated studio barbers may voluntarily connect their own Google account. This keeps confirmed salon bookings synchronized to that barber's Google Calendar in near real time.

  • Google Calendar events: create, update, and delete only calendar events generated from the barber's Blade & Blend Studio bookings.
  • Email address and account identity: show the barber which Google account is connected.

Google access is optional, used only for this staff-facing synchronization feature, and can be disconnected by the barber at any time.
