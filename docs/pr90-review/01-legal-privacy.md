### L1 — P2, reproduced: the legal importer rejects the Worker's own published-page stylesheet

**Introduced here.** `src/admin/cms/nativePages.ts:330-337` requests `/privacy.html` and `/terms.html`, parses the response, and now throws `Den juridiska sidan länkar en okänd stil.` for every stylesheet link except `/tokens.css` and `/info.css`. The base implementation instead fetched same-origin stylesheet links and appended their CSS.

**Actual route:** `src/worker.ts:1068-1071` redirects each `.html` alias to the canonical path. For a published legal CMS page, `src/worker.ts:1135-1190` uses the built `/index.html` shell. `withoutShellBootAssets` (`src/worker.ts:478-523`) removes module scripts/preloads but intentionally retains application stylesheet links. The built shell currently contains `/assets/O871JQ2L.css` (`dist/index.html:161`). The new importer therefore rejects a normal successful response from this application's Worker. It cannot reach the new tokenized static fallback just by asking for `.html`.

**Caller/impact trace:**

- The only direct consumer of `readCorePageSource` is `prepareCorePageSource`, `src/admin/cms/corePages.ts:87-102`; it rejects and resets its loading promise on failure.
- Studio `refresh`, `src/admin/cms/Studio.tsx:145-150`, awaits preparation before `setDraft` when `hasCorePageLayouts` is false. A missing native core layout with an already-published legal page blocks loading the draft and shows the source error (`Studio.tsx:210-214`).

- A brand-new CMS with no published legal pages reaches static assets and avoids the mismatch. A complete fully current document that never requires recapture avoids it too. This limits severity to P2 rather than claiming every Studio visit is broken.

### Official requirements checked (2026-10-27)

- **GDPR Articles 13(1)(a), (c)–(f), 13(2)(a)–(e):** information at collection covers controller identity/contact, purposes and legal basis, relevant legitimate interests/recipients/transfers, retention period or determining criteria, rights, consent withdrawal and complaint rights. Source: IMY's official full-text publication, https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/introduktion-till-gdpr/dataskyddsforordningen-i-fulltext/ (Article 13; retrieved lines 1519–1546). EUR-Lex's current direct HTML/PDF fetch was blocked by its anti-bot page, so the Swedish supervisory authority's full text was used instead. The policy retains all relevant subject areas; actual factual completeness depends on the owner's processing facts.
- **GDPR Article 5(1)(e), storage limitation:** keep identifiable personal data only as long as needed for its purpose, subject to applicable exceptions; IMY recommends deletion/anonymization routines and distinguishes legally required retention. Official source: https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/grundlaggande-principer (storage-minimization section; updated 2026-06-15). There is no universal GDPR rule that all salon bookings must be erased after a particular invented number of days.
- **Swedish Electronic Communications Act, Chapter 9 Section 28:** device storage/access generally requires purpose information and consent; narrow exceptions cover message transmission or what is necessary for the service expressly requested by the user. Primary text: https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-2022482-om-elektronisk-kommunikation_sfs-2022-482/ . PTS official guidance also requires an active choice, rejection as easy as acceptance, and an accessible withdrawal mechanism: https://pts.se/internet-och-telefoni/kakor-cookies/ . This is why optional receipts and necessary authenticated sessions were traced separately; no new violation was established.
- **Google API Services User Data Policy:** clear and accessible disclosure of Google data access/use/storage/sharing, minimum relevant scopes, and adherence to disclosed user-facing purposes; new undisclosed uses require appropriate notice/consent. Source: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy (last updated 2024-02-15, retrieved current). Existing Google disclosure remains intact and the PR does not add scopes or Google-data uses.
- **Supabase currency check:** https://supabase.com/changelog.md returned an unsupported Markdown content-type through search, so https://supabase.com/changelog was read. Relevant recent notices do not change the reviewed consent/notice logic. No Supabase API implementation change was made by this reviewer.

These sources establish the evaluation criteria; they do not certify this business's legal compliance. No invented identity, retention duration, legal basis or transfer mechanism is proposed.
